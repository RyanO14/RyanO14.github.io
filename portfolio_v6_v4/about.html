About

I'm a Bachelor of Engineering student at McMaster University, studying Engineering Physics and Biomedical Engineering with minors in Finance and Nuclear Engineering. My work spans reactor component design, computational multiphysics modeling, and photonics research, with a consistent focus on translating rigorous analysis into clean, well-documented engineering.

Outside of coursework and research, I enjoy playing golf, hockey and skiing. My favourite sports teams are the Montreal Canadians and Las Vegas Raiders.

Please reach out about anything you would like to chat about, even if its not directly related to what I have been working onor even academically related to myself at all!
